# Q3 2024 Revenue Report

## Executive Summary

Acme Corp reported strong financial results for Q3 2024. Revenue grew 15% year-over-year to $42.3 million, driven by expansion in the enterprise segment and higher attach rates on premium subscriptions.

Operating margin expanded by 2.1 percentage points to 18.4%, reflecting improved gross margins from cloud infrastructure optimization and disciplined SG&A spending.

## Revenue Breakdown

Enterprise revenue accounted for 62% of total revenue, up from 55% in Q3 2023. Mid-market and SMB segments contributed 28% and 10% respectively.

Geographic mix: North America 58%, EMEA 27%, APAC 15%. APAC was the fastest-growing region at 28% year-over-year.

## Cost Structure

Costs decreased by 5% year-over-year on a unit-cost basis after normalizing for headcount growth. Cost of goods sold improved due to renegotiated cloud contracts with AWS and Azure, yielding approximately $1.2 million in annualized savings.

Sales and marketing spend remained flat as a percentage of revenue at 22%. R&D investment increased 8% as the company expanded its AI product suite.

## Outlook

Management reaffirmed full-year 2024 guidance of $165–170 million in revenue and 17–19% operating margin. The company expects Q4 to be the strongest quarter seasonally, with enterprise renewals concentrated in December.

Risks include foreign-exchange headwinds in EMEA and potential delays in two large enterprise deals currently in late-stage negotiation.
