# Internal Memo: Cost Efficiency Program — Status Update

**To:** CFO Office  
**From:** Ops Finance  
**Date:** 2024-10-15  
**Subject:** Q3 cost-efficiency program results

## Summary

The cost-efficiency program launched in January 2024 delivered measurable savings in Q3. Total addressable cost base under the program is $28 million. Realized savings for the quarter were $1.4 million (5% of the addressable base), slightly ahead of the $1.2 million target.

## Cloud Infrastructure

Renegotiated AWS reserved-instance commitments and Azure hybrid-benefit licenses. Combined cloud spend declined from $2.1M/quarter to $1.85M/quarter — a $250K quarterly reduction, or ~$1.0M annualized.

## Vendor Consolidation

Three redundant SaaS tools (analytics, support ticketing, design collaboration) were sunset in August. Annualized savings: $180K. One-time migration cost: $40K, fully expensed in Q3.

## Headcount Discipline

Hiring freeze in non-engineering roles held through Q3. Attrition-driven backfills only. Headcount ended the quarter at 412, down 3 from Q2. No material impact on delivery velocity was observed in engineering OKRs.

## Risks and Watch Items

- Cloud savings assume reserved-instance utilization stays above 85%. Current utilization is 91%.
- Vendor consolidation introduced a short-term support backlog; SLA recovery expected by end of October.
- Further savings beyond 5% will require process redesign, not just rate negotiation.
