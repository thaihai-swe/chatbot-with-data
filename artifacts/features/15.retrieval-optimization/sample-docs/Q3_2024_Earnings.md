# Q3 2024 Earnings Report

**Company:** TechCorp Inc.  
**Period:** Q3 2024 (July - September)  
**Date:** October 15, 2024

---

## Executive Summary

TechCorp delivered strong Q3 2024 results with revenue of $2.3B, up 18% year-over-year. Our focus on AI-powered authentication and cloud infrastructure drove significant growth.

---

## Financial Highlights

- **Revenue:** $2.3 billion (+18% YoY)
- **Operating Income:** $450 million (+22% YoY)
- **Net Income:** $320 million (+20% YoY)
- **EPS:** $2.15 (vs $1.79 Q3 2023)

---

## Product Performance

### Authentication Platform
Our new **AI-Enhanced Authentication System** launched in August 2024 exceeded expectations:
- 2.5 million active users (up from 1.8M in Q2)
- 99.99% uptime achieved
- 40% reduction in false positives using machine learning
- Biometric authentication adoption: 65% of enterprise customers

**CEO Statement on Authentication:**
"The AI-Enhanced Authentication System represents a breakthrough in security. By combining behavioral biometrics with adaptive risk scoring, we've created the most secure and user-friendly authentication platform in the market. This is the future of identity verification."

### Cloud Infrastructure
- Revenue: $890 million (+25% YoY)
- New enterprise customers: 450
- Average contract value: $125K
