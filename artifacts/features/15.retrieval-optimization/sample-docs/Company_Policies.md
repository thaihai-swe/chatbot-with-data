## Return and Refund Policy

### Product Returns

**Eligibility:**
- Products may be returned within **30 days** of purchase
- Product must be in original condition with all packaging
- Software licenses are non-refundable after activation
- Custom implementations are non-refundable

**Return Process:**
1. Contact support@techcorp.com with order number
2. Receive return authorization (RA) number
3. Ship product to our returns center within 7 days
4. Refund processed within 10 business days of receipt

**Refund Methods:**
- Original payment method (credit card, wire transfer)
- Processing fee: $50 for hardware returns
- Shipping costs are non-refundable

**Exceptions:**
- Enterprise contracts: Subject to contract terms
- Annual subscriptions: Pro-rated refunds available within first 30 days
- Training services: 50% refund if cancelled 48+ hours before session

### Service Cancellations

**Monthly Subscriptions:**
- Cancel anytime with 30 days notice
- No refund for partial months
- Data export available for 90 days after cancellation

**Annual Subscriptions:**
- Pro-rated refund if cancelled within first 30 days
- After 30 days: No refund, but service continues until end of term
- Auto-renewal can be disabled anytime

**Enterprise Contracts:**
- Governed by individual contract terms
- Typically require 90 days notice for cancellation
- Early termination fees may apply

## Remote Work Policy

### Eligibility

**Fully Remote Positions:**
- Engineering roles (Software, ML, DevOps)
- Product Management
- Customer Success
- Sales (field positions)

**Hybrid Positions:**
- Minimum 2 days/week in office
- Core hours: 10am-3pm local time
- Flexible schedule around core hours

**Office-Required Positions:**
- Executive leadership
- Facilities and operations
- Hardware engineering (lab access required)

### Requirements

**Home Office Setup:**
- Dedicated workspace with door
- High-speed internet (minimum 50 Mbps)
- Ergonomic chair and desk
- Quiet environment for video calls

**Equipment Provided:**
- Laptop (MacBook Pro or equivalent)
- External monitor (up to 2)
- Keyboard and mouse
- Headset with microphone
- $500 home office stipend

**Security Requirements:**
- VPN required for all work
- Encrypted hard drive
- Screen lock after 5 minutes idle
- No public WiFi for sensitive work

### Communication Expectations

**Availability:**
- Online during core hours (10am-3pm local)
- Respond to Slack within 2 hours during work hours
- Calendar must be up-to-date
- Video on for team meetings

**Meetings:**
- All meetings default to video-enabled
- Record meetings for async viewing
- Share notes in Notion within 24 hours

## Vacation and Time Off

### Paid Time Off (PTO)

**Accrual Rates:**
- **0-2 years:** 15 days/year (1.25 days/month)
- **3-5 years:** 20 days/year (1.67 days/month)
- **6+ years:** 25 days/year (2.08 days/month)

**Usage:**
- Minimum increment: 4 hours (half day)
- Maximum carryover: 10 days to next year
- Payout on termination: Unused PTO paid out

**Approval Process:**
1. Submit request in BambooHR at least 2 weeks in advance
2. Manager approval required
3. Blackout periods: End of quarter (last week)
4. Team coverage plan required for absences >5 days

### Holidays

**Company Holidays (11 days):**
- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Presidents' Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving (2 days)
- Christmas (2 days)
- Floating holiday (1 day - your choice)

**Holiday Policy:**
- If holiday falls on weekend, observed on Friday/Monday
- Remote workers observe holidays in their local jurisdiction
- International employees: Local holidays apply

### Sick Leave

**Unlimited Sick Days:**
- No accrual or tracking
- Use as needed for illness or medical appointments
- Doctor's note required for absences >3 consecutive days
- Short-term disability available after 5 consecutive days

**Family Sick Leave:**
- Care for immediate family members
- Up to 5 days/year without documentation
- Additional time available under FMLA

### Parental Leave

**Primary Caregiver:**
- 16 weeks paid leave
- Can be taken within first year
- Flexible return schedule (part-time ramp-up available)

**Secondary Caregiver:**
- 8 weeks paid leave
- Can be taken within first year

**Adoption:**
- Same benefits as birth parents
- Includes foster-to-adopt placements

## Data Privacy Policy

### Employee Data

**What We Collect:**
- Personal information (name, address, SSN)
- Employment history and performance reviews
- Compensation and benefits data
- Time tracking and attendance
- Work product and communications

**How We Use It:**
- Payroll and benefits administration
- Performance management
- Legal compliance
- Security and access control

**Data Retention:**
- Active employees: Duration of employment + 7 years
- Former employees: 7 years after termination
- Exceptions: Legal holds, ongoing investigations

### Customer Data

**Handling Requirements:**
- Access on need-to-know basis only
- No downloading to personal devices
- No sharing outside approved systems
- Encryption required for data at rest and in transit

**Breach Response:**
- Report suspected breaches immediately to security@techcorp.com
- Do not attempt to investigate yourself
- Preserve evidence (don't delete logs)
- Security team will coordinate response

### GDPR Compliance

**Employee Rights:**
- Right to access your data
- Right to correction
- Right to deletion (subject to legal requirements)
- Right to data portability

**Request Process:**
- Email privacy@techcorp.com
- Response within 30 days
- Identity verification required

## Code of Conduct

### Core Values

1. **Integrity:** Be honest and transparent
2. **Respect:** Treat everyone with dignity
3. **Excellence:** Deliver high-quality work
4. **Collaboration:** Work together effectively
5. **Innovation:** Challenge the status quo

### Expected Behavior

**Professional Conduct:**
- Arrive on time for meetings
- Meet deadlines and commitments
- Communicate proactively
- Give and receive feedback constructively
- Maintain confidentiality

**Inclusive Environment:**
- No discrimination based on protected characteristics
- No harassment or bullying
- Use inclusive language
- Respect diverse perspectives
- Report concerns to HR

**Conflicts of Interest:**
- Disclose outside employment
- No competing businesses
- No personal benefit from vendor relationships
- Recuse yourself from decisions where conflicted

### Prohibited Conduct

**Zero Tolerance:**
- Harassment or discrimination
- Violence or threats
- Theft or fraud
- Substance abuse at work
- Sharing confidential information

**Disciplinary Action:**
- First offense: Written warning
- Second offense: Suspension
- Third offense: Termination
- Serious violations: Immediate termination

### Reporting Violations

**How to Report:**
- Direct manager
- HR (hr@techcorp.com)
- Anonymous hotline: 1-800-ETHICS-1
- Online portal: ethics.techcorp.com

**Protection:**
- No retaliation for good-faith reports
- Confidentiality maintained where possible
- Investigation within 10 business days
