# Financial Compliance Guide - Version A

## Corporate Governance Requirements

### Board Composition
- Minimum 3 independent directors
- Maximum board size: 15 members
- CEO and Chair roles must be separate

### Audit Committee
- Must have at least 3 members
- All members must be financially literate
- At least one financial expert required

### Executive Compensation
- Say-on-pay vote required annually
- Golden parachute payments limited to 2.99x base salary
- Clawback provisions for restatements

## Risk Management

### Enterprise Risk Management (ERM)
- Annual risk assessment required
- Board oversight of risk appetite
- Chief Risk Officer reporting directly to board

### Cybersecurity
- Annual penetration testing mandatory
- Incident response plan required
- Board-level cybersecurity expertise recommended

## Compliance Reporting

### Quarterly Filings
- Form 10-Q due 40 days after quarter end
- MD&A must discuss material risks
- Controls testing results disclosed

### Annual Filings
- Form 10-K due 60 days after fiscal year end
- SOX 404 assessment for accelerated filers
- Auditor attestation required for large accelerated filers

# Financial Compliance Guide - Version B

## Corporate Governance Requirements

### Board Composition
- Minimum 5 independent directors (DIFFERENT from Version A)
- Maximum board size: 20 members (DIFFERENT from Version A)
- CEO and Chair roles may be combined with lead independent director (DIFFERENT)
